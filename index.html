Resources & Culture

A small library of facts, proverbs and background — sourced from published Amharic references — to go with your practice.

🔡 34 base charactersThe Fidel (ፊደል) has 34 consonant families, each with 7 vowel orders — so learning the chart unlocks roughly 238 syllable signs at once.
📜 An abugida, not an alphabetAmharic script is an abugida: each symbol is a consonant+vowel pair, and the vowel changes the shape rather than adding a separate letter.
🗓️ Its own calendarEthiopia uses the Ethiopian calendar, which runs about 7–8 years behind the Gregorian calendar and starts its new year (Enkutatash) around September 11.
🌍 Second most-spoken Semitic languageWith over 30 million native speakers, Amharic is one of the most widely spoken Semitic languages in the world, after Arabic.
✍️ Written left to rightUnlike its Semitic relatives Arabic and Hebrew, Amharic is written left to right, similar to English.
🎼 Its own numeral systemGe'ez-derived Amharic numerals look nothing like Arabic numerals — the app currently teaches everyday counting using Arabic numerals, with native numerals planned.

A growing collection of well-known Amharic proverbs (ምሳሌያዊ አነጋገሮች), each with a phonetic reading and its closest English equivalent or meaning. Tap 🔊 to hear a spoken reading.
